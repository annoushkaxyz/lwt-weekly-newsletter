# The Weekend List rules

A standalone checklist for drafting the newsletter and the weekly social and website spin-offs. Follow every item. If a fact cannot be verified, leave it out.

## Tone and sign-off

- Write as a warm local curator, in the tone of The Weekend List #1.
- Keep practical details easy to scan: place, days, times, and price.
- Sign off with: "See you next weekend, Lisbon Weekend Trips."

## Newsletter shape

- Short weekend headline and lede.
- 6 featured picks, then ALSO THIS WEEKEND with about 4 shorter items, for about 10 plans in total. Featured count can run from 6 to 10 if the weekend is unusually full, but the edition should still feel complete, not thin.
- Each featured pick: emoji hook title, 2-3 short paragraphs, practical dates and prices, a `📍 place · days · price` line, and one call-to-action link.
- Keep the ALSO section, and any other secondary section, short.
- Note a major Thursday evening when it belongs with the weekend.

## What belongs on the list

- [ ] The event is real and still on, checked against an official source.
- [ ] The pick has its own website or Instagram.
- [ ] It has not appeared in a previous edition, even as a "last chance" note.
- [ ] If it is a festival or multi-show event, the text is one overview paragraph and the link goes to the full programme.
- [ ] If it is outside Lisbon, it is within about a two-hour drive.
- [ ] It fits the mix: culture, history, nature, festivals, or an under-touristed plan. Not nightlife only.
- [ ] It is not a generic municipal or village fair.
- [ ] Organiser or partner requests already saved for this edition are included.
- [ ] Time-sensitive limits (sold out, last day, registration needed) are flagged.

## Facts

- [ ] No invented events, dates, prices, names, or other facts.
- [ ] Dates, times, and prices match the official page.
- [ ] Claims such as "pets welcome" or "free entry" are checked on the organiser's site, not assumed.
- [ ] Two events are not described as sharing a venue unless that is actually true.

## After the newsletter

For Instagram and TikTok carousels:

- Each pick slide has a catchy emoji title, one sentence of description, and a location line with the event's full official run dates (for example "20 Nov" or "20-26 Nov"), plus place and price when known.
- Follow the slide order the editor has set, even if the newsletter used a different order.

For captions:

- Open with "This weekend in Lisbon and beyond, [dates]."
- Then one line per pick, with a catchy emoji and a short description.
- For Instagram, include @handles taken only from official sites. A pick may have more than one tag.
- For TikTok, use the same caption with no @ tags.
- Close with: "Save this for the weekend. Want the full list in your inbox every week? Subscribe to our newsletter via the link in bio."
- Hashtags: #lisbonweekendtrips #lisbon #lisboa #portugal #thingstodoinlisbon

For the website:

- Update the This Weekend page through a pull request the editor can review.
- Do not merge or publish it unless the editor asks.

## Never

- [ ] Do not publish, email, or create a platform draft unless the editor asks.
- [ ] Do not put private emails, private message contents, or internal identifiers into a public draft.
- [ ] Do not tag an account that is not linked from the official site.
