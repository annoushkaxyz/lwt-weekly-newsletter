## 🎭 D. Maria II is back

After three years closed for renovation, **Teatro Nacional D. Maria II** finally reopens on Rossio this weekend, appropriately with _Macbeth_, the same play that was on stage when a fire devastated the theatre in 1964.

Friday's celebrations spill outside: **Rossio em Festa starts at 21:30**, with videomapping across the theatre façade and DJ sets. On Saturday and Sunday, you can also explore the renovated building on free tours running every 15 minutes from **11:00-15:30**. _Macbeth_ is free but ticketed and currently listed as sold out; check the theatre's page for any same-day availability.

📍 **Rossio · Fri-Sun · opening programme free**
